Backlog

Every change of the project in one table: what it is for, where it stands, and how big it is. Open a row for what it does and its commits.

None of this is ready to merge as it is. Much of it is rough: it works, but it isn't the shape it should have upstream. Nothing goes up until the one before it has been discussed, and picom may decline any of it; then it stays in the fork. The code was written with LLMs; see a note before you read on.

Reading the table#

  • # is a change's place in the fork's branch winanim: the order it was built in, and the order it could go in. Merged changes and those outside picom have none.
  • Size is the lines a change adds, tests and docs included; hover it for the split. XS is under 50 lines, S under 200, M under 600, L under 1,500, XL more. Rows count what their own commits add, so the fork's rows come to 21,898, a little more than the about 21,000 lines the fork adds in all: later commits rework some lines of earlier ones.
  • Outlook is my rough estimate of how far a change is from a shape picom could take. None of them is ready to merge as it is: much of this is rough, and every change would need review, and most of them rework, one at a time. The estimate weighs how general a change is, how big, how much API picom would have to keep, and its known problems. closest (+2): a small fix or a small general mechanism. close (+1): general, but large or new API. open (0): general, with a design question or a known gap. far (-1): shaped for one plugin or style, or at odds with how picom works. hack (-2): works, but by means picom shouldn't carry. Open a row for the reason. The rows outside picom are rated the same way, for their shape. I wrote or oversaw most of this code, so I am not a neutral judge; picom's maintainers are.
  • Touches names what a change adds to that others build on or users write: the plugin API, the backend API, the script language, picom's options, or its D-Bus interface.
  • Status: merged in picom or i3; open a PR is posted; written written and tested, not posted; in fork in the fork, an open question for picom; plugin stays in a plugin; setting a setting, nothing to offer; parked built, kept on a branch, not proposed; dropped tried and let go.
